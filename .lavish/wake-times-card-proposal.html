Lunifer Sleep Insights

Replace sleep-duration history with one wake-times card.

This keeps the app honest about what it knows well: wake time. Instead of emphasizing sleep onset and total sleep duration, the screen would show a focused history of wake times and use that pattern as the trusted signal.

Current

Sleep History + Night Sleep detail

  • Charts estimated sleep duration across ranges.
  • Selected-night detail shows bedtime and wake time.
  • Strong visual weight goes to a number Lunifer may not estimate reliably.
Proposed

Wake Times

  • One card, no separate sleep-history and night-sleep cards.
  • Points represent actual wake clock time, not sleep duration.
  • Detail focuses on the 7-day average, latest wake, and selected morning.

What changes in product behavior

1
The “SLEEP HISTORY” chart becomes “WAKE TIMES.”
2
The selected detail card no longer says “Night Sleep” or shows bedtime, because that invites trust in a weak signal.
3
The top recommended sleep card can stay for now, but this mock assumes the history area becomes one wake-focused card.
4
Empty state becomes “Wake times will appear after a few mornings,” not sleep-data language.
